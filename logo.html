Prior to the rebrand, Boswell Books had multiple logos that they used. In two of these logos, they utilized an illustration of the toad from The Wind in the Willows. The rebranded logo also uses this character, as it is popular with customers. However, the toad is done in a more cartoonish style, which will be more easily remembered by the customer, and has the potential to be utilized in more contexts. This creates a character for the brand, which they can use in other branding. The logo is also more kid-friendly, appealing to families is important to reaching a large market. The toad also has been given a warmer expression, and appears to be extending a book toward the viewer. This creates a more inviting image for the brand. There is the application of color, as opposed to the previous only black, detailed illustration. This use of shape of colors creates a simpler and cleaner logo, which is less intimidating to the viewer, and easily memorable.


Full Color Versions

Primary Mark

The primary logo uses the frog illustration on top of the Boswell word mark, in a vertical formation. The brand mark should not be placed on either side of the word mark.


Secondary Mark

The secondary logo uses the frog illustration to the left of the horizontal, unstacked Boswell title. The brand mark should not be placed to the right, and the text should not be stacked.


Word Mark

The word mark is the stacked version of the Boswell title. The word mark utilizes the font Map Roman Variable Regular, completely capitalized.


Brand Mark

The brand mark is the toad illustration by itself.
